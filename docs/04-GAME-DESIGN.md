# Game Design Document — Mohammadpur Arc

Tone: **Dhallywood action movie meets Dhaka street comedy.** Loud, colourful, absurd, warm. Violence is cartoon-ish; the jokes punch at *behaviours* (chhintai, ghush, VIP culture, traffic chaos, fake vloggers) — never at real people, religions or tragedies.

---

## 1. Story premise

**Mokbul Hossain ("Mokbul")**, 27, ex-bus helper on the Mohammadpur–Motijheel route, gets his phone, wallet and his late father's watch snatched at Asad Gate. The police ask for "chaa-pani" before writing a GD. Mokbul decides to get it back himself — and discovers the snatch is run by an organised ring, **"Chhintai Inc."**, protected by a corrupt OC and bankrolled by a fake-charity politician. One stolen watch becomes a war for the streets of Mohammadpur.

Second protagonist (unlocked mid-Act 1): **Shirin Akter ("Shiri Apa")**, 29, a no-nonsense CNG driver and the fastest wheel in Mohammadpur, whose CNG was "requisitioned" by the same gang.

### Acts (long-term)
| Act | Area | Theme |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mohammadpur (+ edges of Lalmatia, Shyamoli, Asad Gate) | Chhintai Inc. & the corrupt Thana |
| 2 | Dhanmondi, Farmgate, Karwan Bazar | Media, fake news, real-estate mafia |
| 3 | Old Dhaka (Puran Dhaka), Sadarghat | Smuggling, river boats, Shakrain festival |

## 4. Characters (original parody archetypes)

> We do **not** use real influencers, politicians, actors or July-movement participants by name or likeness. Instead, each character is an original *archetype* everyone in Bangladesh will recognise and laugh at — this is funnier, legally safe, and keeps the website from being taken down.

| Character | Role | Archetype / joke |
|---|---|---|
| **Mokbul Hossain** | Protagonist | Ex-bus helper, shouts route names out of habit ("Motijheel! Gulistan! Khali! Khali!") |
| **Shirin "Shiri Apa" Akter** | Protagonist 2 | CNG queen, zero patience, best driver in Dhaka |
| **Chacha Mofiz** | Mentor / info broker | Tea-stall owner who knows every secret; sells "special cha" |
| **Ostad Bulbul** | Melee trainer | Retired Dhallywood stuntman, speaks only in 90s movie dialogue, slow-motion poses |
| **Hero Shakil Khan "Dhishum"** | Comic ally | Fictional washed-up action hero shooting a low-budget film in Mohammadpur; every scene he gets hurt |
| **Villain Kala Kobra** | Film-villain parody | Over-acted Dhallywood-style villain who turns out to be a real gang accountant |
| **Boro Bhai (Jalal Mia)** | Main antagonist | Head of Chhintai Inc., runs a "charity foundation" |
| **OC Kamal Pasha** | Corrupt cop | Never without his tea and an envelope; "Ami kichui dekhi nai" |
| **SI Morshed "Ghush" Ali** | Comic cop | Constantly on the phone with his wife |
| **Jhontu "Tokai" Rana** | Snatcher lieutenant | Fastest motorbike snatcher, mama's boy |

Fictional parties for rally content: **Ghorar Dim Party (GDP)**, **Shonar Pathorbati League**, **Jhalmuri Jonota Front**.

Pedestrian archetypes (for barks & behaviour): fuchka-wala, rickshaw-wala, office-goer, garments worker, madrasa students, aunty with shopping, college couple, delivery rider, beggar-with-QR-code, tong-dokan crowd, wedding band.

### Visual direction (original, not copied)
- **"Golden Dhaka"**: warm humid haze, orange-pink sunsets, teal shadows, heavy bloom on signage at night.
- Dense vertical clutter: tangled electric wires, hand-painted shop signboards (Bangla typography), rickshaw art, banners, water tanks on rooftops, half-finished buildings with rebar.
- Wet monsoon roads with reflections; dust in dry season.
- Stylised-realistic PBR, not cartoon; characters slightly exaggerated for readability.

## 6. Systems design

### 6.1 Thana Alert (wanted system) — original design
| Level | Icon | Response |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 🔔 1 whistle | Nearest foot constable chases, whistles |
| 2 | 🔔🔔 | Patrol motorbikes |
| 3 | 🔔🔔🔔 | Police pickups, armed |
| 4 | 🔔🔔🔔🔔 | Special-unit black SUVs, roadblocks |
| 5 | 🔔🔔🔔🔔🔔 | Helicopter, armoured van, everything |

Lose them by breaking line of sight → "search cone" phase → cooldown. **Bribe option** at levels 1–2: pay Taka to OC's phone number ("bKash koren" parody → in-game "Dhaka Pay") — comedy + money sink.

### 6.2 Economy
- Currency: **Taka (৳)**. Earn from missions, side jobs, robbing gang stashes (never ordinary pedestrians in story missions; free-roam crime raises Thana Alert and lowers Respect with civilians).
- Spend: weapons, ammo, armour, vehicles, garage, outfits, bribes, food (health).
- **Respect** (XP): levels 1–50 unlock weapons, vehicles, outfits.

### 6.3 Vehicles (all original designs, no real-brand logos)
| Vehicle | Feel |
|---|---|
| Pedal rickshaw | Slow, wobbly, hilarious, can carry 2 passengers; tips over on hard turns |
| CNG auto-rickshaw | Nimble, cage doors, weak top speed |
| Motorbike (110cc / 150cc / sports) | Fast, lane-splitting, wheelies |
| Bicycle | Silent, stealthy |
| Private car (hatch, sedan, SUV) | Standard driving |
| Local bus ("Bihongo"-style parody liveries) | Heavy, pushes cars, helper jumps on and off |
| Microbus, Truck, Covered van | Heavy |
| Police pickup, Special-unit SUV | Siren, fast |
| Ambulance | Siren, unlock |

### 6.4 Weapons
Melee: fist, lathi (bamboo stick), cricket bat, hockey stick, brick, machete (no gore), frying pan (joke).
Guns: pistol, revolver, SMG, pump shotgun, assault rifle, sniper rifle (P1), RPG (late unlock).
Throwables: grenade, smoke grenade, tear gas, "cocktail" fire bottle, firecracker (distraction).

## 7. Audio & radio
Original stations (royalty-free/commissioned music, parody DJ talk):
- **Radio Jhaal FM** — Bangla pop & band music, overexcited RJ.
- **Bhaiya Beats** — hip-hop/rap Bangla.
- **Folk Express** — baul / folk-inspired instrumentals.
- **Talk Show "Shotti Kotha"** — fake call-in show about the player's own crimes.
- Fake ads: "Shotti Bolchi Detox Tea", "Plot-e Bari, Bari-te Plot Real Estate", "Ghush-Free Coaching Centre".

## 8. Accessibility
Subtitles (Bangla/English), colour-blind HUD palette, aim assist toggle, hold-vs-toggle options, reduced camera shake, adjustable difficulty.
