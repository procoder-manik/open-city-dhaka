# Game Design Document — Mohammadpur Arc

Tone: **Dhallywood action movie meets Dhaka street comedy.** Loud, colourful, absurd, warm. Violence is cartoon-ish; the jokes punch at *behaviours* (chhintai, ghush, VIP culture, traffic chaos, fake vloggers) — never at real people, religions or tragedies.

---

## 1. Story premise

> Working title: **GTM: Great Thief Mostofa** (name from `/brand.config.json`, changeable). Like the genre it is inspired by, **you play a criminal on the rise**: steal any vehicle, snatch, extort, rob, loot, smash, fight and shoot your way up, and escape the police.

**Mostofa ("Mostu")**, 27, ex-bus helper on the Mohammadpur–Motijheel route. Fired by his owner, cheated by a local *Boro Bhai* who takes his savings as "chanda", and slapped by OC Kamal Pasha for complaining, Mostofa decides: *"Ei shohor-e shot manush-er jayga nai."* He starts small (snatching phones from rickshaw passengers, stealing CNGs), then works for and against rival gangs, extorts shops, robs warehouses and banks, until every thana in Dhaka has his photo on the wall: **Dhaka's Most Wanted**.

Second protagonist (unlocked mid-Act 1): **Shirin Akter ("Shiri Apa")**, 29, getaway driver and CNG queen, the fastest wheel in Mohammadpur.

The comedy comes from how absurd Dhaka's crime world is: bribe-hungry cops, extortion "receipts", gangsters with Facebook pages, fake-charity politicians. All characters, gangs and parties are fictional.

### Acts (long-term)

|---|---|---|

## 3. Side activities (repeatable)

| Activity | Description | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| **Chhintai Run** ⭐ | Snatch bags/phones from rickshaw and CNG passengers, escape the crowd and police. Streak bonus. | Taka, Notoriety |
| **Chanda Collection** | Weekly extortion rounds on your turf; smash shops that refuse. | Passive income |
| **Dokan Lut** | Rob or loot shops, mobile stores, jewellers. | Taka, Thana Alert |
| **Gari Chor Order** | Chop shop wants a specific vehicle (rickshaw, CNG, bike, car, bus) delivered undamaged. | Taka |
| **Turf War** | Take over gang zones street by street. | Territory, income |
| **ATM / Van Dakati** | Hit cash vans and ATMs. | Big Taka, high heat |
| **Street Race** | Bike/car races on Ring Road, Satmasjid Road at night. | Taka, leaderboard |
| **Bounty** | Take out rival gang lieutenants. | Taka, weapons |
| **Rickshaw / CNG jobs** | Legit side jobs ("20 taka beshi" bargaining) when you need to lie low. | Taka, heat cool-down |
| **Pothole Hunter** (collectible) | Find 50 legendary potholes; each has a name. | Achievement |
| **Fuchka Challenge** | Eat-off minigame at a stall. | Stamina buff |
| **Cricket in the Gully** | Quick batting minigame with local kids. | Respect |
| **Traffic Volunteer** | Respectful nod to student volunteers (Aug 2024): direct traffic, clear a jam. Never a target of crime. | Respect, "Joddha" badge |

**Notoriety vs Public Love:** crimes raise Notoriety (unlocks gang jobs, bigger heists); helping people raises Public Love (cheaper prices, people don't call police). Players choose their style.

## 4. Characters (original parody archetypes)

> We do **not** use real influencers, politicians, actors or July-movement participants by name or likeness. Instead, each character is an original *archetype* everyone in Bangladesh will recognise and laugh at — this is funnier, legally safe, and keeps the website from being taken down.

| Character | Role | Archetype / joke |
|---|---|---|
| **Mostofa ("Mostu")** | Protagonist | Ex-bus helper turned **smart young mastan**: fitted jeans, open printed shirt over a black tee, white sneakers, aviator sunglasses, gold chain, trimmed beard, swept hair. Swagger walk, cocky one-liners, secretly soft-hearted. Still shouts bus routes out of habit ("Motijheel! Gulistan! Khali! Khali!") |
| **Shirin "Shiri Apa" Akter** | Protagonist 2 | CNG queen, zero patience, best driver in Dhaka |
| **Chacha Mofiz** | Mentor / info broker | Tea-stall owner who knows every secret; sells "special cha" |

| **Hero Shakil Khan "Dhishum"** | Comic ally | Fictional washed-up action hero shooting a low-budget film in Mohammadpur; every scene he gets hurt |
| **Villain Kala Kobra** | Film-villain parody | Over-acted Dhallywood-style villain who turns out to be a real gang accountant |
| **Boro Bhai (Jalal Mia)** | Main antagonist | Mohammadpur's top mastan, runs a "charity foundation"; Mostofa's first boss and final target |
| **OC Kamal Pasha** | Corrupt cop | Never without his tea and an envelope; "Ami kichui dekhi nai" |
| **SI Morshed "Ghush" Ali** | Comic cop | Constantly on the phone with his wife |

### Visual direction (original, not copied)
- **"Golden Dhaka"**: warm humid haze, orange-pink sunsets, teal shadows, heavy bloom on signage at night.
- Dense vertical clutter: tangled electric wires, hand-painted shop signboards (Bangla typography), rickshaw art, banners, water tanks on rooftops, half-finished buildings with rebar.
- Wet monsoon roads with reflections; dust in dry season.
- Stylised-realistic PBR, not cartoon; characters slightly exaggerated for readability.

## 6. Systems design

### 6.1 Thana Alert (wanted system) — original design
| Level | Icon | Response |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 🔔 1 whistle | Nearest foot constable chases, whistles |
| 2 | 🔔🔔 | Patrol motorbikes |
| 3 | 🔔🔔🔔 | Police pickups, armed |
| 4 | 🔔🔔🔔🔔 | Special-unit black SUVs, roadblocks |
| 5 | 🔔🔔🔔🔔🔔 | Helicopter, armoured van, everything |

Lose them by breaking line of sight → "search cone" phase → cooldown. **Bribe option** at levels 1–2: pay Taka to OC's phone number ("bKash koren" parody → in-game "Dhaka Pay") — comedy + money sink.

### 6.2 Economy
- Currency: **Taka (৳)**. Earn from missions, side jobs, robbing gang stashes (never ordinary pedestrians in story missions; free-roam crime raises Thana Alert and lowers Respect with civilians).
- Spend: weapons, ammo, armour, vehicles, garage, outfits, bribes, food (health).
- **Respect** (XP): levels 1–50 unlock weapons, vehicles, outfits.

### 6.3 Vehicles (all original designs, no real-brand logos)
| Vehicle | Feel |
|---|---|
| Pedal rickshaw | Slow, wobbly, hilarious, can carry 2 passengers; tips over on hard turns |
| CNG auto-rickshaw | Nimble, cage doors, weak top speed |
| Motorbike (110cc / 150cc / sports) | Fast, lane-splitting, wheelies |
| Bicycle | Silent, stealthy |
| Private car (hatch, sedan, SUV) | Standard driving |
| Local bus ("Bihongo"-style parody liveries) | Heavy, pushes cars, helper jumps on and off |
| Microbus, Truck, Covered van | Heavy |

### 6.4 Weapons
Melee: fist, lathi (bamboo stick), cricket bat, hockey stick, brick, machete (no gore), frying pan (joke).
Guns: pistol, revolver, SMG, pump shotgun, assault rifle, sniper rifle (P1), RPG (late unlock).
Throwables: grenade, smoke grenade, tear gas, "cocktail" fire bottle, firecracker (distraction).

## 9. Voices, talk & funny details (see `10-AUDIO-DIALOGUE.md`)
- Every NPC archetype has its own speech style and funny barks: rickshaw-wala ("মামা ২০ টাকা বেশি দিয়েন, রাস্তা খারাপ"), tea chacha, shopkeeper, hawker, aunty, neta (long empty speeches), police ("কাগজপত্র কই?"), foreigners in English ("Is this the way to Dhanmondi? Everything is honking!").
- Mostofa can **greet, threaten or snatch** from anyone, **order or grab food** at stalls; NPCs react with lines + facial emotion.
- Faces: blink, smile, angry brows, mouth moves while talking.
- Funny posters/banners on every wall and pole (original). Examples: "এখানে প্রস্রাব করা নিষেধ — করিলে ৫০০ টাকা জরিমানা", "জিন-পরী তাড়ানো হয় — গ্যারান্টি সহকারে", "হারানো বিজ্ঞপ্তি: আমার মন", "বাসা ভাড়া দেওয়া হইবে — ব্যাচেলর নিষেধ", "ভাইয়ের আগমনে শুভেচ্ছা" (fictional neta Bablu), "এখানে ময়লা ফেলিলে গাধা", "১০০% গ্যারান্টিতে ইংলিশ শিখুন ৭ দিনে".

## 10. Your own Mostofa, Wanted & reputation
- **Name & title:** default **গ্রেট থিফ মোস্তফা**. Signed-in players pick a title (গ্রেট থিফ, রংবাজ, গ্যাংস্টার, মাস্তান, টপ টেরর, চোরের ওস্তাদ… or custom) and may rename the hero. Story lines use `{hero}`, so every subtitle, poster and headline shows *their* name. Pre-generated voices avoid saying the name; lines that contain it use the device voice for custom names.
- **Crime record:** snatch (+1 star), grab food (+0.5), threaten (+0.3), steal a vehicle (+0.5 first time each). Stars cool down by 1 every ~90 s when no new crimes. Total crimes are kept on the record.
- **Wanted posters & headlines:** poles get "ওয়ান্টেড" posters with a mugshot, name, title, reward (৳5,000 × stars) and crimes; roadside billboards show breaking-news headlines ("ব্রেকিং: মোহাম্মদপুরে রংবাজ মোস্তফার ছিনতাই, পুলিশ খুঁজছে!"); a newspaper toast appears in the HUD.
- **Recognition:** at 1+ stars NPCs recognise the hero ("এইটা তো পোস্টারের মোস্তফা!").
- **Bribe:** phone OC Kamal Pasha (fictional) → pay ৳300 × stars → Wanted cleared, with a funny call. Not enough money → he refuses.
- **Good deeds:** greeting, paying, helping earn ❤️ points; 3 points wipe 1 crime off the record (redeem in the pause menu).
- Police chases, arrests and Thana come in Phase 4 and plug into the same Wanted level.
